# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making this project a welcoming and supportive one.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and inclusive in your language and actions.
- Assume good intent, and seek to understand others.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience, technical knowledge, and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behaviour
- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without consent.

## Reporting Issues
If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the [project owner](https://github.com/heinventions).
All reports will be handled confidentially.
